Our Services

PanelPro Auto Spares supports vehicle repair businesses, resellers, and private customers with quality parts and practical automotive supply solutions.

What We Supply

  • Body Panels: Front bumpers, doors, fenders, bonnets, and replacement body components for professional repair and restoration work.
  • Trim & Accessories: Exterior and interior finishing parts that improve the appearance and integrity of the vehicle.
  • Workshop Support: Reliable parts supply for panel beaters, garages, and repair specialists working to tight deadlines.
  • Bulk Orders: Competitive pricing and flexible stock support for resellers, fleet maintenance teams, and trade buyers.
  • Parts Advice: Expert guidance to help customers choose the right component for fitment, quality, and budget.
