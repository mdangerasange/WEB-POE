About PanelPro Auto Spares

We supply dependable automotive parts that help workshops, resellers, and vehicle owners keep their vehicles on the road with confidence.

Our Story

PanelPro Auto Spares was built to solve a common problem in the automotive industry: reliable, quality spare parts that are easy to source and quick to fit. From body panels to trim and accessories, we focus on practical solutions that meet the needs of busy repair businesses and drivers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, high-quality automotive parts with dependable service, fast delivery, and expert support for every customer we serve.
